## 1. Headline

* The data is **thin**: four winners. Of 56 candidate attributes, 9 are STRONG, 9 CONDITIONAL, 38 REJECTED under the brief's §5.2 rules, and most "strong" ones are noise-prone (see §8).
* The card has **4 factors** (brief allows 4–6; only four survive every filter) and covers four causal domains.

Top half 3/4, top 5 in 2/4, top 3 in 2/4, compression gate 4/4 — minimums met, but the §8 permutation control shows a selection procedure run on random winners does as well or better (69% of shuffles reach 4/4 top-half), so these results are **not evidence of skill**.

## 2. Data and quality notes

* All 8 RFB files used (484,246 runner rows, 1 Jan 2022 – 31 Dec 2025). Cesarewitch IDs 44842126 / 45213126 / 45577128 / 45941119 → 21 / 31 / 24 / 19 runners (95).
* Files are UTF-8 bytes decoded as Latin-1 (fractions arrive as `Â½`); the parser strips `Â`. All `DstBtn` values now parse (0 failures). Beaten distance in features uses `TotalBtn` (cumulative).
* Class values above 6 (47, 50 — 67 rows) are treated as missing. Irish races mostly lack Class → NA.
* **History censoring.** RFB starts 1 Jan 2022, so "career" means career-since-Jan-2022: ~9 months of history for 2022 runners, ~21 / ~33 / ~45 months for 2023 / 2024 / 2025. * Banned from the factor search per §4.2: OR as score input, weight, draw, trainer, jockey, price/market, going, course form. `or_entry` is carried as a reference column only; `or_chg3` (OR change) was a candidate but is OR-derived and 8–29% NA.

## 4. Factor selection

Procedure applied to the STRONG/CONDITIONAL, correlation-surviving candidates:

1. **Field-wide support filter (declared before weighting; grounds: brief §7.3 "must separate across the field distribution, not just at the winner's data point").** Require Field-wide ρ ≥ +0.05 in the winner direction. This removes `l_yards` (ρ 0.00), `r2_btn` (−0.01) and `l_field` (−0.06): they separate the four winners from medians but carry no signal across the other 91 runners. This is an addition to §5.2, flagged here.
2. **One fact, one home / domain coverage.** `f_runs16` (STRONG, ρ 0.06) is the same domain as `f_winrate16` (career staying record) and was not admitted. `form_trend3` (ρ 0.11) contains the latest finish, so `l_pprop` (ρ −0.66 with it) was not also admitted. `dual_career`/`j_any` correlate ≈1.0; `dual_career` separates better.
3. `or_chg3` (OR-derived, up to 29% NA) not admitted.

Admitted:

| Factor | Domain | Verdict | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Staying win rate (`f_winrate16`) | Career staying record | STRONG | best separator; ex-2022 84th pctile 3/3 |
| Jumps experience (`dual_career`) | Dual-purpose profile | STRONG | largest field-wide ρ (0.31); 2022 winner is the one without, but his history is censored |
| Wins in last 12 months (`wins_365d`) | Current form / momentum | CONDITIONAL | 2/4 consistency; included at reduced weight by the effect formula |
| Form trajectory (`form_trend3`) | Form trajectory | STRONG (all-years) / REJECTED ex-2022 | **weakest factor**; ρ 0.11 |

Domains covered: career staying, dual-purpose profile, momentum, trajectory (≥3 required ✓).

**Not met: §6.1(4)** (one factor extractable from the Cesarewitch race record alone). The only race-record-only attributes (age; last Form figure; Form-string trend; days) all fail the §5.2 criteria (e.g. `rr_form_trend3`: 56th percentile, 2/4). Admitting one would break the "data beats headlines" rule, so the card requires RFB history for all four factors. Missing evidence is handled as NA with active-field mean imputation (never zero) and the card is labelled PROVISIONAL for that runner.

### Selection-bias permutation test

The Phase 2 selection (top four non-rejected attributes by effect, generic percentile scoring) was run on randomly assigned winners (300 shuffles, within-race):

```
observed mean winner rank pct / top-half rate: [0.223 1.   ]
null mean rank pct: mean 0.179  5th pct 0.072
P(null mean rank pct <= observed) = 0.727  n=300
P(null top-half rate >= observed) = 0.693


```

**Reading: the in-sample result is not evidence of predictive skill.** Running the same selection on randomly assigned winners gives a mean winner rank percentile of 0.18 (better than the real card's 0.22), and a null card reaches a top-half rate of 4/4 in 69% of shuffles. With ~50 candidate attributes and four winners, a selection procedure can almost always find four attributes that fit those winners. (The test uses generic within-race percentile scoring rather than the banded card, and no field-wide filter, so it is a proxy, but the gap is too large for that to rescue the result.) The only features with independent support are those with field-wide signal across all 95 runners (jumps experience ρ 0.31, staying win rate 0.16), which is why the field-wide filter was applied. Do not size bets on this card on the strength of the backtest; the first live renewals are the real test.

## 16. Known limitations (read before use)

1. Four winners. Weights are unstable under leave-one-year-out; treat the point values as a ranking device, not calibrated probabilities.

4. No race-record-only fallback exists; runners without RFB history are PROVISIONAL.
5. Race-day overlays (draw, going, course form, trainer/jockey, pace, equipment) remain outside the 100.
